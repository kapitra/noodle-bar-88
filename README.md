**Noodle Bar 88** — fast noodles, happy customers, big tips. Chef Bimo runs the busiest noodle bar on the street: grab ingredients from the crates, chop chicken, veggie and chili on the boards, cook rice, noodle, egg and chicken in the woks, build each dish on a plate, and ring the bell at the pickup counter before the customer's patience runs out.

The whole game is about **speed and satisfaction**: every order ticket shows the customer's face — smiling, neutral, or frowning — as their patience drains, and the live tip it will pay right now. Serve fast and you pocket a fat tip plus a speed bonus; serve slow and the tip shrinks or the customer walks out. Fast consecutive serves build a combo that multiplies your pay. One shift lasts 3 minutes, and the lunch rush only gets busier.

Dishes on the menu: Sate Ayam, Nasi Goreng, Mie Goreng, Ayam Geprek, and Mie Ayam.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Move with WASD / arrow keys or by clicking, use a station with E / Space or by clicking it. P pauses, M mutes, R restarts. No network requests, no build step — the game is static files under `game/`, all art drawn in canvas.
